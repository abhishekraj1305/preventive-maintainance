How these numbers work

Downtime includes only time overlapping this period, including ongoing breakdowns. MTTR averages repair-start to repair-end for repairs closed in this period. MTBF divides recorded operating hours by breakdowns started in the period, and requires an entry for every applicable day. An em dash means the metric cannot be calculated.

PM counts refer to task occurrences, not machines. Compliance requires approval and an actual completion time before the end of the original due date; future deadlines are excluded.
